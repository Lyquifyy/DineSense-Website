The day comes with you.

Three moments bring the daily budget to dinner while keeping the choice yours.

  1. 01

    Before the menu

    Set today’s context.

    See the budget you set for the day, with an optional amount for the meal you’re choosing.

  2. 02

    At the restaurant

    Find a dish that fits your night.

    Browse a restaurant’s menu and compare up to three picks. Existing menu details and manual entry work without AI.

  3. 03

    When you decide

    Bring your choice back to the day.

    Confirm your choice to update the running daily totals. The budget is there to inform, never to score the meal.

Nutrition, with its source in view.

When restaurant nutrition is published, DineSense shows it as published. When it’s estimated, the app labels it clearly and keeps the range visible.

Values shown here are illustrative examples, not restaurant data or nutrition advice.
