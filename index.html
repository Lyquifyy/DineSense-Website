A few things
before dinner.

What do I need?

An iPhone with iOS 26+ and a DineSense account. Your first search and order are free. Continued use requires DineSense Pro; plans are shown in the app.

Does it work with my food tracker?

Copy a picked meal’s calories and macros into your tracker. DineSense doesn’t log meals automatically or keep a food diary.

Will every restaurant have a menu?

Coverage varies. Find a missing menu online or photograph it in the app. Those dishes are marked as estimates.
