# DineSense website

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users and purpose

People deciding what to order at restaurants, equally including people who bring a meal's calories and macros from a tracker and people who choose Lighter, Regular meal, or More protein without setting numbers. The website explains the current SwiftUI iPhone app and invites visitors to request access to its small TestFlight pilot.

## Capabilities and constraints

The app finds and ranks dishes near the phone, shows nutrition provenance, supports order changes, keeps saved dishes and picks, copies numbers into a tracker, and displays an order on the Lock Screen. Published nutrition and estimates stay visibly distinct. DineSense keeps no food diary. Apple Health is optional and read-only. An account and DineSense Pro are required; pricing is shown in the app. It runs on iPhone with iOS 26 or later. Do not claim Android availability, a public release, automatic tracker sync, restaurant ordering/payment, or universal menu coverage.

Product truth comes from the current DineSense SwiftUI implementation, PRODUCT.md, and release records in the sibling DineSense repository. The old website's daily-budget, three-dish comparison, and Android descriptions are retired.

## Evidence

Website previews use real screens captured from the current app in its deterministic demo mode, labeled as demo data. They demonstrate UI, not live menu coverage or deployed-backend acceptance. Capture provenance and refresh steps live in assets/SOURCES.md. Do not invent testimonials, adoption figures, endorsements, or nutrition accuracy guarantees.

## Brand commitments

DineSense's plate-and-spark mark, Fraunces display type, warm black, cream, sage, and restrained brass. Warm, exact, unhurried copy. User-approved dark cinematic presentation, actual app previews, and guided scroll motion. Both audiences receive equal prominence.

## Delivery

Static HTML, CSS, and JavaScript on the existing hosting setup. Keep index.html, privacy.html, support.html, CNAME, and .nojekyll. Pilot requests open email to dinesense.feedback@gmail.com; no submission backend or new analytics. Support and Privacy inherit the shared appearance; substantive privacy disclosures are preserved.
