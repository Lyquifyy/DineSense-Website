About DineSense

DineSense is an iPhone app for deciding what to order at a restaurant. You bring a meal’s calories and protein, or pick Lighter, Regular meal or More protein. DineSense finds nearby dishes that fit and shows the exact order to ask for.

What does DineSense do?

It ranks dishes near you against the meal you have in mind. You can change an order, save dishes you like, and copy a meal’s numbers into your food tracker. Your order can also sit on the Lock Screen while you wait in line. See how DineSense finds an order, step by step.

Where do the numbers come from?

When a restaurant publishes nutrition, DineSense uses it and links to the source. When it doesn’t, dishes come from a menu found online or a menu photo, and those numbers are marked as estimates. The restaurant order guides show the same method worked out by hand, row by row.

What doesn’t DineSense do?

DineSense doesn’t keep a food diary or log meals to your tracker automatically. It doesn’t place restaurant orders or take payment. Not every restaurant has a menu yet. Apple Health access is optional and read-only. The DineSense privacy policy lists what the app collects and who helps run it.

Where can I get DineSense?

DineSense is on the U.S. App Store for iPhone with iOS 26 or later. Your first search and order are free with an account; continued use requires DineSense Pro.

How do I contact DineSense?

Email dinesense.feedback@gmail.com for account help, a menu correction, privacy questions or feedback. For subscriptions and refunds, see DineSense support.
