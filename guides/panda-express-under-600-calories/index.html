A Panda Express order under 600 calories

Pair a published serving of Grilled Teriyaki Chicken with a full Super Greens side. The two rows add up to 460 calories and 46 g protein.

What to order

Grilled Teriyaki Chicken with Super Greens as the side. The calculation uses 6.1 oz chicken and 10 oz Super Greens, with no additional sauce packet, appetizer or drink.

460 calories · 46 g protein · 30 g carbs · 18 g fat

Before you order

Panda also lists Super Greens as an entrée and in a Cub Meal. Those are different portions. This order uses the full 10 oz side, not either of those smaller rows.

Extra sauce packets, appetizers and drinks are not counted. The published chicken row is used as listed; no sauce calories have been subtracted from it.

Menus, availability and portions can change. These are published figures for standard servings, not a measurement of your meal. Ask the restaurant about allergens and the portion you receive.
