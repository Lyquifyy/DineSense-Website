DineSense / Support

We can help.

For account help, a menu correction, or feedback from the pilot, email the DineSense support inbox. If a dish’s numbers look wrong, you can also open the dish in the app and tap “Report a menu issue.”

Email support ↗

When reporting a menu issue, include the restaurant and dish name and the figure you think needs checking. Do not include a password.

If you used Sign in with Apple and deleted your DineSense account, you can also stop using DineSense with Apple in iPhone Settings > your name > Sign in with Apple. Select DineSense and tap Delete.
