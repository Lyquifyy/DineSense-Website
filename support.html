We can help.

For account help, a menu correction, or feedback, email the DineSense support inbox. If a dish’s numbers look wrong, you can also open the dish in the app and tap “Numbers look wrong? Report it.”

Email support ↗

When reporting a menu issue, include the restaurant and dish name and the figure you think needs checking. Do not include a password.

DineSense Pro

DineSense Pro is billed through your Apple Account. To change plans or cancel, open iPhone Settings > your name > Subscriptions > DineSense, or DineSense > Settings > Manage subscription. Cancel at least a day before it renews. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com. On a new phone, sign in to the same DineSense account, or tap Restore purchases on the subscription screen.

Deleting your DineSense account, in Settings or from the subscription screen’s Account button, disconnects Sign in with Apple. It does not cancel DineSense Pro; cancel that in your Apple Account as above. The privacy policy lists what deleting your account removes.

Where do a dish’s numbers come from?

Published nutrition comes from the restaurant and links to its source. Numbers from a menu found online or a menu photo are marked as estimates. The restaurant order guides show the arithmetic for three complete orders, and the app walkthrough shows where each number appears.
