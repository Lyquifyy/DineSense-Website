DineSense / Privacy

Privacy, in plain view.

Last updated October 2, 2026

DineSense helps you choose restaurant meals against a meal target. This policy explains what the iPhone app handles and how to contact us.

Information you give us

An account has a user ID and, if you link an email or Sign in with Apple, an email address. We keep your meal targets, food preferences, saved dishes, picks, and the nutrition snapshot recorded with each pick. Restaurants and menu items you add by hand or from a photo are private to your account. We also keep menu correction reports you send in the app.

Menus, questions, and location

When you photograph a menu, the image is sent through our menu-reading service to process it; DineSense does not store the photo as a menu image in your account. Menu searches and questions may use Anthropic to read menus and write short explanations. Nearby search uses Apple Maps on the device. A rough location may be sent to Google Places to help find a restaurant photo; DineSense does not save that position. Published chain nutrition and verified map places can be shared across accounts.

Apple Health

Apple Health access is optional. If you turn it on, DineSense reads your day’s nutrition on your iPhone to suggest a target, and can write a meal after you choose it. Health intake read from Apple Health is not sent to our server. You can change Health permissions in iPhone Settings.

Usage and diagnostics

We keep limited search and pick events to measure whether the pilot works, without storing the words you searched for. Crash and hang diagnostics can be associated with your account to help fix problems. DineSense does not use your data for advertising or cross-app tracking.

Services and deletion

Supabase hosts account and app data. Anthropic processes menu and question requests; Google Places provides restaurant photos; Apple supplies Maps, Sign in with Apple, and optional Health access. We use these services to run the features you request.

You can delete your account in DineSense Settings. Deletion removes account data and your private restaurants and menu entries. A separate person’s saved meal or order keeps its own nutrition snapshot if it previously referred to a dish you contributed. If you used Sign in with Apple, you can also remove DineSense from your Apple Account’s Sign in with Apple settings after deletion. Contact us for privacy or deletion questions.
